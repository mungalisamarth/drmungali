About the Kutir

The Neem Karoli Maharaj Kutir is a sanctified room dedicated to Neem Karoli Baba (Maharajji), kept in the warmth of his blanket and the silence of his presence. Long held privately at Dr. Mungali's residence, the Kutir is now opened — modestly, carefully — to devotees who wish to chant, sit in satsang, or simply share a few quiet moments in Maharajji's grace.

The Kutir is not a temple or an ashram in the public sense. It is a living, devotional space — a small room with the capacity to hold about twenty people in stillness. From here, we live-stream SundarKand, Hanuman Chalisa and Vinay Chalisa every Sunday, host the Divine Nectar Seekers' Q&A every Wednesday, and gather periodically for satsang with Dr. Mungali. Visits are warmly welcomed by prior appointment.
