How do you tell the story of a saint who never preached?

To tell it, we sit with two people who knew him closely, were graced by him and still carry his presence in their lives. Then we listen.

At the heart of In the Presence of Maharaj-ji is a long, intimate conversation between Krishna Das and Dr. Nirmal Mungali. They remember Maharaj-ji through laughter, silence, regret, song and stories carried for a lifetime. He is not treated as a figure from the past. We come to understand how his presence continues in the people he touched.

The film does not ask anyone to adopt a belief. It simply invites the audience to listen and spend time with two people who were changed by him.

“The story is alive. You become part of it.” Dr. Nirmal Mungali

What the film is about

In the Presence of Maharaj-ji is a feature-length documentary about Neem Karoli Maharaj and the presence he continues to have in the lives of those who knew him.

The story is grounded in the friendship and conversation between Krishna Das and Dr. Mungali. Dr. Mungali remembers Maharaj-ji through family, home, medicine and a lifetime of personal stories. Krishna Das speaks about closeness, separation, regret, surrender and the chanting that became his service. Maharaj-ji is the bond between them.

At its heart, the film asks how someone can leave the body and still remain present. The answer comes through the people he loved, the ways their lives changed and what they chose to do with that love.

The storytellers

These two voices carry the film. Their memories, side by side, are what the whole story is built from.

Storyteller

Krishna Das

Krishna Das is one of the world's most recognised voices of kirtan and a Grammy-nominated recording artist. He travelled to India in 1970 and spent two and a half years with Neem Karoli Maharaj, who gave him the name Krishna Das.

Krishna Das speaks here as a disciple. He remembers the closeness of being with Maharaj-ji, the pain of being sent away, the regret of not returning when called and the surrender that later shaped his chanting. His story shows how a private relationship became a lifetime of service.

Biography reference: Krishna Das official biography, krishnadas.com.

Storyteller

Dr. Nirmal Mungali

Dr. Nirmal Mungali grew up in Haldwani in a family that knew Maharaj-ji closely. Maharaj-ji called him Nimmi. He carries personal memories that are sacred, playful and sometimes difficult.

His story moves through family, childhood, medicine and a lifetime of knowing Maharaj-ji. As a doctor, he also reflects on the limits of knowledge and on the experiences that changed his understanding of healing. Through Divine Nectar, he continues to share lesser-heard Leelas of Maharaj-ji with new generations.

In active development

Where the film stands

In the Presence of Maharaj-ji is currently in active development.

We have filmed an extensive fireside conversation between Krishna Das and Dr. Mungali. The fundraising trailer is complete, and a detailed story treatment lays out the structure of the feature film.

The next phase includes focused filming in India, selected follow-up conversations, editing and post-production.

We are expecting to release the film in early 2027. We will share updates as the work progresses.

What support enables

Filming in India

Story-essential homes, roads, temples, ashrams, kitchens, landscapes, objects and present-day acts of service.

Focused follow-up filming

Concise pickups with Krishna Das, Dr. Mungali and selected witnesses where the story requires clarity or emotional completion.

Archival research and permissions

Family photographs, documents, historic images, audio and other material that can be verified and legally cleared.

Editing and story construction

Shaping extensive conversation footage and new material into a disciplined feature-length film.

Post-production

Sound edit and mix, colour grade, music clearances, subtitles, graphics, mastering and quality control.

Release preparation

Legal review, insurance and clearances, festival and platform deliverables, publicity assets and audience materials.
