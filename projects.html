Neem Karoli Baba Himalayan Sanctuary

A vision in making

The vision is to establish a contemplative spiritual sanctuary in the Kumaon Himalayas, rooted in silence, inquiry, and service. The land is intended to hold the living presence of Neem Karoli Baba. This sanctuary is not conceived as a destination for spiritual tourism, but as a space deliberately protected from noise, trend, and performance. It is meant for those who seek genuine inner depth, whether through sustained engagement, periodic visits, or quiet connection from afar.

The intention is to create something pristine, enduring, and quietly transformative. A place where the land, the silence, and the rhythm of daily life naturally invite reflection, healing, and inner growth, without obligation or display.

This vision calls for patrons and well-wishers who feel drawn to support it through generous and meaningful contribution. Such support is essential to build and protect a sanctuary of this nature, one that remains independent, disciplined, and true to its purpose. This is an opportunity to help anchor a sacred space in the Himalayas that serves not only those who arrive, but also those who remain connected to it in spirit.

Divine Chants app

Sundar Kand, Hanuman Chalisa, Tulsidas Ramayan and more

Divine Chants is a science based chanting experience designed to help devotees to enter each verse with clarity, rhythm, and understanding.

Each chant unfolds verse by verse through authentic recitation, guided text, and traditional folk style artwork inspired by classical devotional imagery.

Created for both first time chanters and lifelong devotees, Divine Chants bridges ancient devotion with modern attention, transforming chanting into a lived, meditative experience.

Feature Documentary on Neem Karoli Maharaj

A conversation on grace, love, and lived devotion

In the Presence of Maharaj-ji is a contemplative documentary shaped as an intimate fireside conversation between Krishna Das and Dr. Mungali. Rather than explaining Neem Karoli Baba through chronology or doctrine, the film allows understanding to arise through lived experiences, personal stories, moments of silence, and devotional music. It unfolds like a satsang, inviting the viewer to encounter Maharaj-ji not as a figure of the past, but as a living presence.
